Is Vessel Clean safe to take every day?

Yes. It is made from four kitchen ingredients and balanced so one tablespoon a day sits easily on the stomach. If you are pregnant, diabetic, or take blood-thinning or other regular medicine, ask your doctor first.

Does it taste strong or bitter?

No. The raw honey rounds off the garlic and vinegar, so it tastes warm and tangy rather than harsh. Mix one tablespoon in a glass of lukewarm water and it goes down easily.

How long until I notice something?

Most customers tell us they feel lighter after meals in the first week and notice steadier energy in two to three weeks of daily use.

How do I take the RISE and GLOW shots?

Shake the small bottle, drink it in one go first thing in the morning, then lift the bottle to read the message hidden under it. Keep the box in the fridge.

How is it delivered and how do I pay?

We ship across Pakistan by courier, usually within 2 to 4 days, and delivery is free. You pay the rider in cash when the parcel arrives.

What if my bottle arrives damaged?

Message us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we send a free replacement. We do not take returns for change of mind, which keeps every bottle you receive untouched and fresh.
